> Source: CareerForge Complete Requirements Package v1.0. Companion documents: see /architecture folder for technical design

## 2. User Personas
### Persona A — "Amara," University Student (3rd year, Information Systems)
- **Goals:** Build a first-ever professional resume for internship applications; look credible despite limited work history.
- **Frustrations:** Doesn't know what counts as an "achievement" versus just a task; intimidated by blank templates; has done coursework projects but never framed them as resume content.
- **Key needs:** Strong Projects and Skills sections carrying more weight than Experience; guidance/prompts, not just empty fields.

### Persona B — "Daniel," Recent Graduate (0–1 years experience)
- **Goals:** Apply to multiple entry-level roles across slightly different tracks (e.g., IT support vs. junior developer).
- **Frustrations:** Maintains three separate Word documents already and dreads updating all three every time he adds a certification.
- **Key needs:** Multiple resumes from one profile; fast duplication and re-targeting.

### Persona C — "Grace," Experienced Professional (8+ years, multiple roles)
- **Goals:** Consolidate a long, fragmented history into a clean, current resume; tailor emphasis by role.
- **Frustrations:** Long experience list with many achievements per role — hard to keep concise per resume without rewriting from scratch each time.
- **Key needs:** Granular achievement selection per resume (show 3 of 10 per role); reordering; a Classic/Executive template.

### Persona D — "Leo," Creative Professional (graphic design / digital media)
- **Goals:** A visually distinctive resume that reflects design sensibility, alongside a plain ATS version for large-company applications.
- **Frustrations:** Design-forward resumes routinely get mangled or rejected by ATS parsers; needs both, from one data set.
- **Key needs:** Strong template/theme customization; ATS mode as a genuine one-click alternate output, not a compromise on the main template.

### Persona E — "Priya," Active Job Seeker (applying to many roles concurrently)
- **Goals:** Maintain 5+ live resume variants tailored to different postings, updated constantly.
- **Frustrations:** Losing track of which resume version was sent where; forgetting to add a new skill to all variants.
- **Key needs:** Resume list/dashboard clarity; job description comparison to quickly check fit before tailoring; fast duplication.
